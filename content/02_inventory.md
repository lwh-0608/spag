## 一、物料总览：按对成交的直接程度分三层 / 1. Asset Map: three tiers by distance to closing

CSCV 推广所需物料共 18 项，按对成交的直接程度分为三层：第一层用于客户已经开口之后的成交动作，第二层用于让读者愿意开口，第三层用于让陌生人看到。层内按优先级排列，层间不替代：没有第三层就没有线索，没有第二层就没有信任，没有第一层就落不了单。
CSCV promotion needs 18 assets. They sit in three tiers by how directly they move a deal: the first tier supports closing once a prospect has opened a conversation, the second makes readers willing to open one, and the third puts the work in front of strangers. Within a tier the order is priority; between tiers nothing substitutes—no reach means no leads, no trust material means no conversations, and no closing tools means no signed work.

编号说明：A 为客户资料卡；B 为员工资料；S 为八项新增资产；M 为已有的两份长文档。事实表（第〇节）是内部前置项，改价先改它，不作为对外物料。
Numbering: A for client cards, B for staff profiles, S for the eight further assets, M for the two existing long documents. The fact sheet (section 0) is the internal prerequisite: change it first, and do not publish it as an asset.

### 第一层 · 成交工具 / Tier 1 — Closing tools

客户已经开口、正在评估或准备付款时使用。这一层的每一件物料都直接回答“下一步做什么、多少钱、多久交付”。
Used once a prospect has opened a conversation and is evaluating or preparing to pay. Every item here answers “what happens next, at what price, by when”.

**A4 · 一页式邮件 / PDF 留资 · One-page email or PDF leave-behind** — 用途：初次接触后发送、展会留资、报价附件 ｜ 形式：六段编号短文档，纯文字排版 ｜ 平台与字数：邮件正文或独立 PDF，约 200 词（CN 450–650 字 / EN 1,150–1,450 字符） ｜ 负责：客户开发负责人 ｜ P0

Purpose: sent after first contact, used as a trade-show leave-behind or attached to a quote. Form: six numbered sections in a text-only layout, about 200 words. Owner: Client Development Lead. Priority: P0.

**S4 · 方案模板 · Proposal boilerplate** — 用途：把询盘转成范围清楚的报价 ｜ 形式：六段固定结构（范围、不包含项、时间、费用、交付物、免责） ｜ 平台与字数：邮件或 PDF ｜ 负责：客户开发负责人 ｜ P0

Purpose: turns an inquiry into a clearly scoped quote. Form: six fixed sections. Owner: Client Development Lead. Priority: P0.

**S6 · 销售一句话 · Sales one-liners** — 用途：应对五个高频异议（太贵、自己能查、供应商不让查、先做一单、能不能便宜） ｜ 形式：5 组标准问答 ｜ 平台与字数：口头、聊天与邮件通用 ｜ 负责：客户开发负责人 ｜ P1

Purpose: answers the five common objections. Form: five standard Q&A pairs. Owner: Client Development Lead. Priority: P1.

**S5 · 案例模板 · Case template** — 用途：让同类买家看到别人怎么做决定 ｜ 形式：六字段匿名案例 ｜ 平台与字数：文档或邮件 ｜ 负责：客户开发负责人 ｜ P1

Purpose: shows comparable buyers how the decision was made. Form: a six-field anonymized case. Owner: Client Development Lead. Priority: P1.

**S7 · 术语表 · Glossary** — 用途：避免客户把核验当成认证 ｜ 形式：四个词的双语释义 ｜ 平台与字数：官网、PDF 与邮件 ｜ 负责：运营协调员 ｜ P2

Purpose: stops clients reading verification as certification. Form: bilingual definitions of four terms. Owner: Operations Coordinator. Priority: P2.

### 第二层 · 信任材料 / Tier 2 — Trust materials

读者已经看到我们、但还没有开口时使用。这一层回答“你们是谁、凭什么、和别的机构有什么不同”。
Used when a reader has noticed us but has not yet made contact. This tier answers who we are, on what basis, and how we differ.

**A1 · LinkedIn 公司页 About · LinkedIn company page About** — 用途：海外采购在搜索时判断是否值得联系 ｜ 形式：公司页 About 段落 ｜ 平台与字数：LinkedIn；EN 2,600 字符 / CN 1,100 字 ｜ 负责：合伙人 + 客户开发负责人 ｜ P0

Purpose: lets overseas procurement decide whether to reach out. Form: company page About section. Owner: Partner + Client Development Lead. Priority: P0.

**A2 · 微信 / 中文社媒贴文 · WeChat and Chinese social post** — 用途：让货代、律所、贸易服务商愿意转发与引荐 ｜ 形式：长帖，可直接发公众号或朋友圈 ｜ 平台与字数：微信公众号、朋友圈、行业群；CN 450–700 字 ｜ 负责：客户开发负责人 ｜ P0

Purpose: gives forwarders, law firms and trade service providers something to forward. Form: a long post for a WeChat account or Moments. Owner: Client Development Lead. Priority: P0.

**B1 · 员工资料包 · 创始合伙人 · Staff package — Founder and Managing Partner** — 用途：个人号用第一人称讲清楚为什么做这件事 ｜ 形式：Headline + About + 职责范围 + Skills ｜ 平台与字数：LinkedIn、微信、邮件；Headline ≤220 字符 ｜ 负责：合伙人本人 ｜ P0

**B2 · 员工资料包 · 核验主管 · Staff package — Head of Verification** — 用途：让客户相信证据标准与现场能力 ｜ 形式：同上 ｜ 平台与字数：同上 ｜ 负责：核验主管本人 ｜ P0

**B3 · 员工资料包 · 客户开发负责人 · Staff package — Client Development Lead** — 用途：让买家知道第一次沟通会发生什么 ｜ 形式：同上 ｜ 平台与字数：同上 ｜ 负责：客户开发负责人本人 ｜ P0

**B4 · 员工资料包 · 通用团队模板 · Staff package — Generic team template** — 用途：新员工按实际职责填写，不必另写 ｜ 形式：同上，含方括号占位 ｜ 平台与字数：同上 ｜ 负责：每位员工 + 运营协调员 ｜ P0

A staff profile uses first person singular, a headline of up to 220 characters, an About of four to five paragraphs, three to four role-scope bullets and no more than ten skills. Owner and priority are listed with each profile in section 3.

**S1 · 置顶贴文 · Pinned post** — 用途：长期置顶，替代反复解释服务是什么 ｜ 形式：约 800 字/字符的发布贴 ｜ 平台与字数：LinkedIn 或公众号置顶 ｜ 负责：客户开发负责人 ｜ P1

Purpose: pinned long-term so the page stops re-explaining itself. Form: a launch post of about 800 characters. Owner: Client Development Lead. Priority: P1.

**M1 · CSCV 服务说明 v2（已有）· CSCV Service Definition v2 (existing)** — 用途：长文档底稿，一页看懂服务 ｜ 形式：13 页 A4 文档 ｜ 平台与字数：PDF ｜ 负责：合伙人 ｜ 已有

**M2 · CSCV 服务演示 v2（已有）· CSCV Service Deck v2 (existing)** — 用途：面谈、路演与合作方介绍 ｜ 形式：19 页演示 ｜ 平台与字数：PPTX 与 PDF ｜ 负责：合伙人 + 客户开发负责人 ｜ 已有

The two long documents are the existing basis for everything in this file. They are not rewritten here; when a fact changes, both must move to the same version as this kit.

### 第三层 · 触达入口 / Tier 3 — Reach and entry

让陌生人第一次看到我们，并知道从哪里进来。这一层不解释服务细节，只负责把人带到第二层。
Puts the work in front of strangers and shows them where the door is. This tier does not explain the service; it moves people into Tier 2.

**A3 · X / Twitter 单条贴文 · X / Twitter single post** — 用途：触达采购与供应链从业者 ｜ 形式：单条贴文 ｜ 平台与字数：X、Threads、行业社群；EN 280 字符 / CN 140 字 ｜ 负责：合伙人 ｜ P1

Purpose: reaches sourcing and supply chain practitioners. Form: a single post. Owner: Partner. Priority: P1.

**S2 · 微信菜单 · WeChat menu** — 用途：让新访客自助找到服务、价格与联系 ｜ 形式：三个按钮，各配一句说明 ｜ 平台与字数：微信公众号菜单 ｜ 负责：运营协调员 ｜ P1

Purpose: lets a new visitor find the service, the price and the contact route unaided. Form: three buttons, one line of copy each. Owner: Operations Coordinator. Priority: P1.

**S3 · 邮件签名 · Email signature** — 用途：每封对外邮件都带一句定位 ｜ 形式：2–3 行签名，中英各一行 ｜ 平台与字数：所有对外邮件 ｜ 负责：每位员工 + 运营协调员 ｜ P1

Purpose: puts the positioning on every outbound email. Form: a two-to-three-line signature, one CN line and one EN line. Owner: each staff member + Operations Coordinator. Priority: P1.

**S8 · 落地页文案 · Landing page copy** — 用途：承接 A1 与 A3 带来的流量，给一个明确行动 ｜ 形式：首屏一句话 + 三个证据块 + 一个行动按钮 ｜ 平台与字数：官网或落地页 ｜ 负责：客户开发负责人 + 设计排版 ｜ P2

Purpose: catches traffic from A1 and A3 and offers one clear action. Form: a hero line, three proof blocks and one call to action. Owner: Client Development Lead + Design. Priority: P2.

建议使用顺序：先做 P0（事实表、A1–A4、B1–B4），再做 P1（S1–S3、S5、S6），最后做 P2（S7、S8）。同一层内优先做能直接进入客户对话的物料；每件物料的认领、状态与截止日期，用第七节最后一页的清单表。
Suggested order of work: build P0 first (the fact sheet, A1–A4, B1–B4), then P1 (S1–S3, S5, S6), then P2 (S7, S8). Within a tier, build first whatever enters a live client conversation fastest; claim each item, its status and its due date on the checklist table on the last page of section 7.
