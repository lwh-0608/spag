### 员工发布规范 / Staff Posting Rules

1. 只用本人可以核实的内容；不写无法核实的从业年限、头衔与成果。
   Use only what the individual can verify; no unverifiable years in business, titles, or results.

   Split the roles: personal profiles carry judgment and field work; the company page carries the offer and pricing.
3. 同一事实在不同平台保持一致；价格、时限、邮箱以事实表为准。
   Keep the same fact consistent across platforms; price, turnaround, and email follow the fact sheet.
4. 发布前逐条对照事实表与本文档；拿不准的句子删掉，不修改事实去适配句子。
   Check every line against the fact sheet before posting; when in doubt, cut the sentence, never bend the fact to fit it.
5. 不承诺结果、不称认证、不提客户名称、不用感叹号与绝对化用语。
   No promised outcomes, no certification claims, no client names, no exclamation marks, no absolutes.
