## 四、八项新增建议
以下八项文本资产与 A、B 两组材料互补，成本低、复用率高。编号为建议补齐顺序，优先级按对成交的直接程度单独标注。
These eight text assets complement the client cards and staff profiles above. They cost little and reuse existing copy. The numbers are the suggested build order; each asset's priority is marked separately by how directly it moves a deal to close.
### 01 置顶贴文 / Pinned post

用途 / Use：把 A3 扩写成一条约 800 字的完整贴文并长期置顶，让第一次到访主页的人一次读完服务、价格、证据与联系方式，减少反复解释。 / Expand A3 into one full post of about 800 Chinese characters and pin it, so a first-time visitor reads the service, price, evidence and contact in one pass and the page stops re-explaining itself.

形式 / Form：纯文本，6–8 个短段，中文约 800 字、英文约 1,600–2,000 字符；置顶于 LinkedIn 公司页与微信朋友圈首条，不配图也能读。 / Plain text in six to eight short paragraphs, about 800 Chinese characters and 1,600–2,000 English characters; pinned on the LinkedIn company page and at the top of WeChat Moments, readable without images.

要点 / What to write：
- 沿用 A3 的钩子与行动号召，只加证据细节，不加新承诺。 / Keep the hook and the call to action from A3; add evidence detail, not new promises.
- 第一段用一个具体场景（例如工厂发来的车间视频），第二段一句话说清 JFC 是谁、在哪、只为买方工作。 / Open with one concrete scene, such as a factory video sent by a supplier; use the second paragraph to say who JFC is, where it is and that it works only for the buyer.
- 用三段分别写 A-Report（含套餐有效价）、B-Report、客户拿到什么；价格与时限逐字照事实表。 / Use three paragraphs for the A-Report including package effective prices, the B-Report, and what the client receives; copy prices and turnaround from the fact sheet word for word.
- 写一条范围诚实：每份报告逐条列出未核验项；五条独立性承诺至少写出前两条。 / State scope honesty: every report lists unverified items line by line; include at least the first two of the five independence commitments.
- 结尾给一个动作和一个入口：付款前联系我们，发供应商名称与订单金额；落款写邮箱与广州南沙。 / Close with one action and one entry point: contact us before payment with the supplier name and order value; sign off with the email and Nansha, Guangzhou.

负责 / Owner：客户开发负责人起草，合伙人核对事实与口径 / The client development lead drafts; the partner checks facts and wording

优先级 / Priority：P1

### 02 微信菜单 / WeChat menu

用途 / Use：把公众号底部三个菜单做成固定入口，让关注者与转介绍方两步内找到服务、价格与联系方式。 / Turn the three bottom-menu buttons of the WeChat official account into fixed entry points, so followers and referrers reach the service, the price, and the contact in two taps.

形式 / Form：三个按钮，按钮名不超过 8 个汉字，每个按钮配一句说明；示例格式为「按钮名｜一句说明」，左半填后台按钮名，右半放自动回复或链接页第一行。 / Three buttons, each label no longer than eight Chinese characters and each with one line of copy; the sample uses the format "label | line", where the left side is the backend label and the right side goes into the auto-reply or the first line of the linked page.

要点 / What to write：
- 核验服务：说明写「付款前到现场核实主体、产能与风险」，并链接 A1 全文。 / Verification: use "on-site checks of the entity, capacity, and risk before you pay" and link to the full A1 text.
- 价格与流程：说明写两类报告的价格与时限，再给七步交付的链接。 / Pricing and process: give the price and turnaround of both report classes, then link to the seven-step delivery.
- 联系我们：电话与邮箱，地址放链接页；并写清下一步只需供应商名称与订单金额。 / Contact: phone and email, with the address on the linked page; state the next step, which needs only the supplier name and order value.
- 三个按钮按客户决策路径排序：先看服务，再看价格，最后联系；不放促销或文章合集。 / Order the buttons by the client's decision path: service first, price second, contact last; no promotions or article lists.
- 按钮名与说明只写服务、价格与联系方式，不写资质或结果类承诺，不放感叹号。 / Keep labels and copy to the service, the price, and the contact; make no credential or outcome claims, and use no exclamation marks.

### 04 方案模板 / Proposal boilerplate

用途 / Use：把每次报价的固定结构写死，客户在方案第一页就看到范围与不包含项，减少后续争议与反复改稿。 / Lock the fixed structure of every quote so the client sees scope and exclusions on page one, which cuts later disputes and redrafts.

形式 / Form：一页 A4 纯文本，六个固定小节（01–06），编号与标题不变，正文按单填写；附在报价单或服务协议之前。 / One A4 page of plain text in six fixed sections numbered 01–06, with the numbering and headings unchanged and the body filled per engagement; place it ahead of the quote or the service agreement.

要点 / What to write：
- 01 范围：写站点数、报告类型、核验层级（A 为全项 L2，B 为可观察 L1），并写明只给到访时间窗、不提前告知具体日期。 / 01 Scope: number of sites, report class, level of scope (full L2 for A, observable L1 for B), and a visit window with no exact date given in advance.
- 02 不包含项：逐条列出不采购、不谈价、不做质量检验、不发货、不介绍供应商收佣金；B-Report 中因对方未同意而无法核验的项目也写在这里。 / 02 Exclusions: list line by line that JFC does not source, negotiate, inspect quality, ship, or introduce suppliers for a commission; add the B-Report items that could not be verified because consent was not given.
- 03 时间与 04 费用：A-Report 5 个工作日、1,800–3,000 元/站点；B-Report 7–10 个工作日、3,200–4,500 元/站点；套餐 3 站点 4,500 元、5 站点 7,200 元；100% 预付。 / 03 Timeline and 04 Fee: A-Report 5 business days at RMB 1,800–3,000 per site; B-Report 7–10 business days at RMB 3,200–4,500 per site; packages at RMB 4,500 for 3 sites and RMB 7,200 for 5 sites; 100% prepayment.
- 05 交付物：8–12 页报告、带定位与时间戳照片包、原件影像、分层访谈记录、风险评级、范围声明、有效期与免责声明、合伙人签发。 / 05 Deliverables: an 8–12 page report, a geo-tagged and time-stamped photo pack, original document images, layered interview notes, a risk rating, a scope statement, a validity period and disclaimer, and partner sign-off.
- 06 免责：报告只反映核验当日观察到的事实，不构成认证、担保或对未来交易的承诺；模板只定结构，具体条款以签署的服务协议为准。 / 06 Disclaimer: the report reflects only facts observed on the day of verification and is not a certification, a guarantee, or a promise about future transactions; the template sets structure only, and the signed service agreement governs the terms.

负责 / Owner：合伙人定条款，客户开发负责人按单填写范围与报价 / The partner sets the terms; the client development lead fills in scope and quote per engagement

优先级 / Priority：P0

### 05 案例模板 / Case template

用途 / Use：把已完成的项目整理成可对外发布的脱敏案例，作为方案与销售的证据；没有真实项目就不写。 / Turn completed engagements into redacted cases usable in proposals and sales conversations; do not write one without a real engagement.

形式 / Form：一页一个案例，六个字段，中文 200–400 字，中英各一版；全部匿名，不出现客户名、品牌名、城市或合同号。 / One case per page in six fields, 200–400 Chinese characters, one version in each language, fully anonymized with no client name, brand, city, or contract number.

要点 / What to write：
- 行业与订单金额区间：只写行业、买方规模区间（如「消费电子，核心员工 50–200 人」）与订单金额（如首单 USD 20,000 以上）。 / Industry and order value band: give the sector, the buyer's staff range (for example "consumer electronics, 50–200 core staff") and an order value (for example, a first order above USD 20,000).
- 核验模式：写 A-Report 或 B-Report、站点数、是否获得同意、实际交付用了几个工作日。 / Mode: state A-Report or B-Report, the number of sites, whether consent was obtained, and how many business days delivery took.
- 发现：只写报告中的事实与风险评级，包括列为未核验的项目；不写推测与因果。 / Finding: only the facts and risk rating recorded in the report, including the items listed as unverified; no speculation and no causal claims.
- 客户决策与结果：写到动作层面，例如追问、改付款条款、暂缓付款、换供应商；不写「避免损失 XX 万」这类无法核实的数字。 / Client decision and outcome: describe the action, such as follow-up questions, revised payment terms, a payment pause, or a change of supplier; avoid unverifiable figures such as "losses avoided".
- 脱敏与真实：必须脱敏，不得虚构案例；每个案例都要能对应到原始报告与证据包，由合伙人核对后才能使用。 / Redaction and truth: anonymize, never invent a case; every case must trace to an original report and evidence pack, checked by the partner before use.

负责 / Owner：核验主管提供脱敏事实，合伙人签发并审定脱敏度 / The head of verification supplies redacted facts; the partner signs off and approves the level of redaction

优先级 / Priority：P1

### 06 销售一句话 / Sales one-liners

用途 / Use：给五个高频异议各配一句可直接复述的标准回答，电话、微信、邮件里用同一套口径。 / Give the five most common objections one standard answer each that can be repeated verbatim on the phone, in WeChat, or by email.

形式 / Form：五组，每组一句话，中文 40–80 字（不含异议标签），先接住异议，再给事实与下一步；中英各一版，附在报价邮件或销售卡片背面。 / Five lines of 40–80 Chinese characters each (objection label excluded): acknowledge the objection, then give the fact and the next step; one Chinese and one English version, kept with the quote email or on the back of the sales card.

要点 / What to write：
- 每组先承认对方判断合理（「理解」「可以」），再给一个事实，不辩解。 / Open each line by acknowledging the concern, then give one fact; do not argue.
- 事实只锚定价格、时限、单边收费、未核验清单四项，答完给一个下一步。 / Anchor only in four facts, which are price, turnaround, one-sided fee, and the gap list, and end with a next step.
- 不承诺结果、不涉资质与排名、不点名其他机构。 / Promise no outcome, make no credential or ranking claims, and do not name other firms.
- 五个问题顺序固定：太贵、我们自己能查、供应商不让查、先做一单试试、能不能便宜点；同一答复跨渠道不改写。 / Keep the five objections in this order: too expensive, we can check ourselves, the supplier refuses, let us try one order first, can you discount; do not rewrite the same answer across channels.

示例文案（中文）/ Sample copy (Chinese)：
```
1 太贵：理解。A-Report 每站点 1,800–3,000 元、5 个工作日；建议先核马上要付款的那一家，再看是否继续。
2 我们自己能查：可以，公开记录您也能查；我们补的是只有到现场才看得到的部分，包括场地、生产活动、付款主体，并留下带定位与时间戳的照片。
3 供应商不让查：那就走 B-Report，不通知对方，只做可观察项，7–10 个工作日，3,200–4,500 元/站点，报告逐条列明未核验项。
4 先做一单试试：可以。先核一个站点，A-Report 5 个工作日、1,800–3,000 元；多站点可用套餐，3 站点 4,500 元、5 站点 7,200 元。
5 能不能便宜点：理解。价格按站点与报告类型定，A-Report 1,800–3,000 元/站点；套餐 3 站点 4,500 元、5 站点 7,200 元；100% 预付，交付即收款，不再另打折。
```

### 07 术语表 / Glossary

用途 / Use：在第一次沟通与方案附件里把四个常被混淆的词讲清楚，让客户明白核验报告不是认证，范围以报告为准。 / Define the four terms clients most often confuse, so they see that a verification report is not a certification and that the report's scope governs.

形式 / Form：四组词条，每条 2–3 句，先给本项目的定义，再给边界；中文版在前、英文版在后，可放在官网 FAQ 第一屏与方案附件末页。 / Four entries of two to three sentences each: definition first, boundary second; Chinese version first, English version second, for the first screen of the website FAQ and the last page of a proposal annex.

要点 / What to write：
- verification 是本项目的核心：到现场记录事实、做判断，不签发证书、不担保结果。 / Verification is the core of this service: record on-site facts and make a judgment, without issuing a certificate or guaranteeing an outcome.
- inspection 指产品与货物的检验：JFC 不做质量检验，也不出具检验结论。 / Inspection covers product and cargo: JFC does not inspect quality and issues no inspection conclusion.
- audit 指依据准则对财务或体系出具意见：JFC 不是审计机构，不引用审计准则。 / Audit is an opinion on financials or a management system against standards: JFC is not an audit firm and does not invoke audit standards.
- certification 指认证机构按标准签发证书：JFC 不做认证，报告只陈述核验当日的事实。 / Certification is a certificate issued by a certification body against a standard: JFC does not certify, and the report states only the facts observed on the day of verification.
- 四个词在全部材料中保持同一译法，不用「审核」「认证」来指代 verification。 / Keep one rendering per term across every asset; never use "audit" or "certification" to mean verification.

示例词条（中文）/ Sample entries (Chinese)：
```
核验 verification：到现场记录可观察的事实，判断这家企业是否真实存在、是否具备生产能力、风险在哪里。我们不签发证书，不担保交易结果。
检验 inspection：针对产品或货物的质量与数量，按抽样标准执行。JFC 不做质量检验。
审计 audit：依据既定准则对财务或体系出具意见。JFC 不是审计机构，不引用审计准则。
认证 certification：由认证机构按标准签发证书。JFC 不做认证，报告只陈述核验当日的事实。
```

示例词条（英文）/ Sample entries (English)：
```
Verification: record observable facts on site and judge whether the entity is real, whether it can actually produce, and where the risk sits. We issue no certificate and guarantee no outcome.
Inspection: checking product or cargo quality and quantity against a sampling standard. JFC does not inspect quality.
Audit: an opinion on financials or a management system against defined standards. JFC is not an audit firm and does not invoke audit standards.
Certification: a certificate issued by a certification body against a standard. JFC does not certify; the report states only the facts observed on the day of verification.
```

负责 / Owner：合伙人定定义与边界，运营协调员统一排版 / The partner sets definitions and boundaries; the operations coordinator applies one layout

### 08 落地页文案 / Landing page copy

用途 / Use：把 A1 的结构搬到官网首页或独立落地页，让第一次到访的买家在一屏内看懂服务并留下联系方式。 / Move the A1 structure onto the website home page or a standalone landing page, so a first-time buyer understands the service within one screen and leaves contact details.

形式 / Form：按三段写，从上到下：首屏一句话、三个证据块、一个行动按钮；纯文字即可先上线，图片与照片包后补。 / Write it in three parts, top to bottom: a hero line, three proof blocks, and one call to action; plain text can ship first, with images and the photo pack added later.

要点 / What to write：
- 首屏：主标题用「付款之前，把“能不能生产”变成事实。」，副标题写独立核验机构、广州南沙、只向买方收费，再给一行 A-Report 5 个工作日、1,800–3,000 元/站点。 / Hero: use "Before payment, turn 'can they produce?' into a documented fact." as the headline; the subhead gives independent verification firm, Nansha, Guangzhou, paid only by the buyer, plus one line on A-Report at 5 business days and RMB 1,800–3,000 per site.
- 证据块一「我们到过现场」：三个问题、带定位与时间戳的照片包、原件影像，链接到 A1 的对应段落。 / Proof block one, "We went on site": the three questions, the geo-tagged and time-stamped photo pack, and original document images; link to the matching A1 passage.
