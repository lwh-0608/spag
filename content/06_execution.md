## 五、执行建议：分工、排期与节奏 / 5. Execution: owners, schedule and cadence

### （一）先冻结事实表 / Freeze the fact sheet first

价格与时限出现在几乎每一件物料里。先由合伙人确认第〇节事实表，再由运营协调员锁版；之后任何人不得在单个物料里改数字，只能改事实表，再全量同步。两份长文档（M1、M2）与本文件必须同一天更新到同一版本。

Prices and turnaround appear in almost every asset. The partner confirms the fact sheet in section 0; the Operations Coordinator then locks it. After that, no one changes a number inside a single asset—change the fact sheet, then update everything. The two long documents (M1, M2) and this file must move to the same version on the same day.

### （四）发布节奏 / Publishing cadence

- LinkedIn：每周两帖，一条现场记录、一条观点；创始人个人号先发，公司页转发。两帖都从 A1 的事实与结构里取素材，不另造事实。
- LinkedIn: two posts a week, one field note and one opinion. The founder's personal profile posts first, the company page reshares. Both draw facts and structure from A1; no new facts are invented.
- 微信：每周一篇长文（A2 的变体），朋友圈与行业群转发短段落；转介绍方私信优先发 A4 与 S7。
- WeChat: one long article a week (a variant of A2), with short paragraphs forwarded to Moments and industry groups. For referral partners, send A4 and S7 first.
- X / Threads：每周两到三条 A3 类型的单条贴文，一条只讲一个事实。
- X / Threads: two or three A3-type single posts a week, one fact per post.
- 面谈与展会：A4 + M2；现场留资当天发出 A4，24 小时内回复能否核验、报价与交付日期。
- Meetings and trade shows: A4 plus M2. Send A4 the same day, and reply within 24 hours with feasibility, price and delivery date.

### （五）A/B 测试开场 / A/B test the opening

用一个月测试两种开场：一种从现场证据切入（照片、记录、付款主体），一种从定金损失切入（付款前那一周）。只改开场，事实、价格与承诺保持不变。判断标准是有效对话数（对方回了实质问题），不是点赞与转发。一个月后保留带来对话的开场，把另一个存档。

Test two openings for a month: one that starts from on-site evidence (photographs, records, payee entity), one that starts from the cost of a bad deposit (the week before payment). Change only the opening; keep the facts, prices and commitments fixed. Judge by the number of substantive conversations, not by likes or reshares. After a month, keep the opening that produces conversations and archive the other.

### （六）版本与同步 / Versioning and sync

文件命名：`CSCV_推广物料_纯文本_CN-EN_v2`；每次改版升版本号并在文末记录变更。任何一次价格、时限或承诺调整，触发全量同步：事实表 → A1–A4 → B1–B4 中涉及价格的句子 → S1–S8 → M1、M2。同步完成后由运营协调员在第 7 节清单上重新勾选。

File naming: `CSCV_推广物料_纯文本_CN-EN_v2`. Raise the version number on every revision and log the change at the end of the file. Any change to price, turnaround or a commitment triggers a full sync: fact sheet → A1–A4 → the price sentences in B1–B4 → S1–S8 → M1 and M2. When the sync is done, the Operations Coordinator re-checks the section 7 checklist.

### （七）每月复盘三个数 / Review three numbers every month

每月只看三个数：有效对话数（哪一层物料带来）、发出的报价数、成交或明确拒绝数。哪个物料连续两个月没有带来对话，就改开场或停用；不要用“再发一段时间看看”替代判断。

Track three numbers a month: substantive conversations (and which tier produced them), quotes sent, and closed or clearly declined. If an asset produces no conversation for two months, change its opening or retire it. “Let us give it more time” is not a decision.
