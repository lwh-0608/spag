## 七、团队清单（最后一页）/ 7. Team checklist (last page)

用法：每件物料一行，负责人认领后填「状态」与「截止」；状态用 ☐ 未开始 / ◐ 进行中 / ☑ 完成。依赖项没完成前不开始下游物料。全部 ☑ 之后由运营协调员归档本版，并启用下一版。
How to use: one row per asset. The owner claims a row and fills in status and due date; status is ☐ not started / ◐ in progress / ☑ done. Do not start an asset before its dependency is done. When every row is ☑, the Operations Coordinator archives this version and starts the next one.

### 发布前最终检查 / Final pre-publish check

每件物料上线前逐条打勾；任一项不通过则不上线。
Tick every line before any asset goes live; if one fails, it does not go live.

- ☐ 事实表已冻结，本物料所有数字、时限、承诺与事实表逐字一致。
- ☐ The fact sheet is frozen and every number, turnaround and commitment in this asset matches it word for word.
- ☐ 中英两版事实一致、结构对应；英文已由母语水平审校过。
- ☐ The Chinese and English versions carry the same facts and structure; the English has been read by a native-level reviewer.
- ☐ 无“认证、保证、最、第一、唯一、领先”等表述；无客户名称与可识别案例。
- ☐ No certification, guarantee, superlative or exclusive claim; no client names or recognizable cases.
- ☐ 无未经标注的行业估算与规划性倍数。
- ☐ No unlabeled industry estimates or planning multiples.
- ☐ 方括号占位已填写并确认；个人简介已由本人确认。
- ☐ Every bracketed placeholder is filled in and confirmed; the person has confirmed their profile.
- ☐ 字数在平台上限内；邮箱与电话正确。
- ☐ Length is within the platform limit; email and phone are correct.
- ☐ 案例与照片已脱敏；未出现正在执行的 B-Report 信息。
- ☐ Cases and photographs are anonymized; no information about an in-progress B-Report appears.
- ☐ 版本号与变更记录已更新；旧版本已停用并从共享目录移除。
- ☐ The version number and change log are updated; the old version is retired and removed from shared folders.
