## 一、物料总览：按对成交的直接程度分三层 / 1. Asset Map: three tiers by distance to closing

CSCV 推广所需物料共 18 项，按对成交的直接程度分为三层：第一层用于客户已经开口之后的成交动作，第二层用于让读者愿意开口，第三层用于让陌生人看到。层内按优先级排列，层间不替代：没有第三层就没有线索，没有第二层就没有信任，没有第一层就落不了单。

They sit in three tiers by how directly they move a deal: the first tier supports closing once a prospect has opened a conversation, the second makes readers willing to open one, and the third puts the work in front of strangers. Within a tier the order is priority; between tiers nothing substitutes—no reach means no leads, no trust material means no conversations, and no closing tools means no signed work.

## 六、使用与合规提醒 / 6. Usage and compliance notes

发布前逐条核对，任何一条不通过就不要发。
Check every item before publishing; if one fails, do not publish.

### （一）可以说什么 / What may be said

- 说事实：在哪个时点、到过什么地方、看到了什么、哪些项没能核验。
- State facts: at what point, what was visited, what was seen, what could not be verified.
- 说范围：核验范围、交付物、时限、价格以事实表为准。
- State scope: verification scope, deliverables, turnaround and price follow the fact sheet.
- 说边界：不采购、不谈价、不做质量检验、不发货、不介绍供应商赚佣金。
- State the boundary: we do not source, negotiate, inspect quality, ship, or introduce suppliers for a commission.
- 说独立性：只向委托方收费，不向被核验方收取任何费用、返点或礼品。
- State independence: we are paid only by the commissioning party and take no fee, rebate or gift from the subject.

### （二）不可以说什么 / What may not be said

- 不称“认证 / certification”，不承诺交易结果，不写无法核实的从业年限，不提客户名称与可识别案例。
- Never call the service a certification, never promise an outcome, never state unverifiable years in business, and never name a client or a recognizable case.
- 不使用“最、第一、唯一、领先、最佳、国家级”等绝对化或排他性用语。
- Never use “best”, “first”, “only”, “leading” or any superlative or exclusive claim.
- 不写“保证通过”“百分百安全”“零风险”之类的担保性表述。
- Never write guarantees such as “guaranteed to pass”, “100% safe” or “zero risk”.
- 不引用未经标注的行业估算与规划性倍数（如 73%、20–100 倍）。
- Do not cite unlabeled industry estimates or planning multiples (such as 73% or 20–100×).

### （三）个人简介与员工资料 / Staff profiles

- 个人简介中的履历与专业背景必须由本人逐句确认；方括号占位内容不得原样发布。
- Every line of employment history and background in a profile must be confirmed by that person; bracketed placeholders must never be published as-is.
- 个人号用第一人称单数，公司页用第一人称复数；个人简介不重复公司页内容，只写本人负责什么、怎么判断、怎么联系。
- Personal profiles use first person singular; the company page uses first person plural. A profile does not repeat the company page—it says what the person owns, how they judge, and how to reach them.
- 员工离职或职责调整时，当天停用其个人简介与邮件签名，并由运营协调员更新 B1–B4 与清单。
- When a staff member leaves or changes role, retire their profile and signature that day and have the Operations Coordinator update B1–B4 and the checklist.

### （四）案例与证据 / Cases and evidence

- 案例只能来自真实项目；一律匿名，隐藏客户名称、国家、品牌、金额细项、工厂名称与可反推的信息。
- Cases may come only from real engagements; always anonymize them, hiding client names, countries, brands, exact amounts, factory names and anything that allows re-identification.
- 案例中的“发现、决策、结果”必须与项目记录一致；不得虚构或拼接不同项目。
- The finding, decision and outcome in a case must match the project record; never invent or merge different projects.
- 正在执行的 B-Report 不得出现在公开文案里：B-Report 的前提是不惊动被核验方，公开讨论即破坏前提。
- Never mention an in-progress B-Report in public copy: a B-Report assumes the subject is not alerted, and public discussion breaks that assumption.
- 对外引用报告时，必须同时给出范围声明与有效期；报告只反映核验当日观察到的事实。
